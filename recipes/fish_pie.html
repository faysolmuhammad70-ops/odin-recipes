Fish Pie

Discription

Fish pie is a savoury pastry snacks made from flour-based dough filled seasoned fish and other ingredients. The filling may contain sardines, mackerel, or other type of fish mixed with onoins, pepper, and spicies. The dough is shaped around the filling and baked until the outer crust become golden brown.

Fish pie is a popular snack in Nigeria and commonly sold in bakeries, food shops, and roadside snack outlets. It can be eaten as a breakfast, a light meal, or a snack between meals. Its cripsy outer crust tasty fish filling make it a delicious and satisfying snack.

Ingredients for Prepering Fish Pie

  • Flour.
  • Water.
  • Magarine or butter.
  • Salt.
  • Baking powder.
  • Egg (optional, for brushing the surface).

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients.
  2. Prepare the fish.
  3. Prepare the filling.
  4. Allow the filling to cool.
  5. Prepare the dough.
  6. Add water.
  7. Roll the dough.
  8. Cut the dough.
  9. Add the filling.
  10. Seal the pies.
  11. Prepare for baking.
  12. Bake the pies.
  13. Allow them to cool.
  14. Serve
Note: Baking time may vary depending on the size of the pies and the type of oven used.
