Jollof Rice

Discription

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made from rice cooked ina rich, spicy tomato and pepper sauce. it is prepared using ingredients such as tomatoes, red ball peppers' onions, vegetable oil, seasoning cubes, salt, and spicies. The rice absorbs the flavour of the sauce as it cooks, giving it its charateristic redish-orange colour, delicious taste, and pleasant aroma.

Jollof rice is commonly served at parties, weddings, celebration, and family gatherings in Nigeria and other West African countries. It can be enjoyed with fried chicken or plantian, beef, fish, or salad. In Nigeria, jollof rice is especially loved for its rich flavour and is often prepared for special occasions as well as everyday meals.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers
  • Onions
  • Tomato paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • Water
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Scotch bonnet pepper

Steps

  1. Wash the rice.
  2. Prepare the pepper mixture.
  3. Heat the oil.
  4. Fry the onions and tomato paste.
  5. Cook the pepper mixture.
  6. Add seasoning.
  7. Add water.
  8. Add the washed rice
  9. Cook the rice.
  10. Check for doneness
  11. Allow it to steam
  12. Serve
