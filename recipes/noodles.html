Instant Noodles

Discription

Noodles are a popular food made from flour, water, and other ingredients, depending on the type. They are usually quick and easy to prepare by boiling them in water and adding seasonings, vegetables, eggs, or meat. Noodles are enjoyed by people of different ages because of their delicious taste and versatility.

In Nigeria, instant noodles are commonly eaten as braekfast, lunch, or dinner. They can be prepared with ingredients such as onions, pepper, eggs, vegetables, and seasoning. Noodles are often served hot and can be enjoyed as a complete meal or as a side dish.

Ingredients for Prepering Noodles

  • Instant noodles
  • Water
  • Onions
  • Fresh pepper
  • Vegetable oil
  • Seasoning powder (usually provided in the noodle pack)

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients.
  2. Boil the water.
  3. Add the noodles.
  4. Add the seasoning.
  5. Add the vegetables.
  6. Add eggs (optoinal).
  7. Cook the noodles.
  8. Check the taste
  9. Serve
